🧠 The Big Picture

Training an AI model is like teaching a child to read — but at scale. The model is fed billions of words, learns patterns through trial and error, and gradually builds a mathematical understanding of language.

This interactive guide breaks down the 8 core steps of training a large language model (like ChatGPT). Each step is visualised with animated diagrams to help you see the process in action.

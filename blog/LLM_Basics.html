Foundations

Large Language Models (LLMs)

An LLM is a highly advanced computer system trained to look at a sequence of words and predict the most logical next word.

  • Tokenization: Slicing text into smaller chunks called "tokens" and turning them into numbers.
  • Parameters: The internal settings ("brain cells") of the AI that it fine-tunes during training.
  • Inference: The processing loop where you give the AI a prompt and it calculates the answer.
Simple Analogy Think of a super-advanced version of your phone's predictive text. Instead of guessing the next word based on the last thing you typed, it reads thousands of books to figure out exactly what should follow your sentence.

The Core Engine

The Transformer Architecture

The Transformer is the framework that makes modern AI possible. Its superpower is a mechanism called Self-Attention.

  • Parallel Processing: Older systems read sentences word-by-word. Transformers look at an entire sentence all at once.
  • Self-Attention: The AI calculates mathematical links between all words simultaneously to understand true context.
Simple Analogy When you read "bank" in "The bank was muddy from the river," your brain connects "bank" to "river." Self-attention lets the AI highlight and connect related words across a whole page instantly.

Retrieval Networks

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

An AI's knowledge is locked at training time. If you ask about recent events or private files, it may hallucinate. RAG solves this.

  • User Prompt: You ask the AI a question.
  • Search & Match: A system searches a private database for matching data before answering.
  • Augment & Generate: Those facts are pasted into the prompt so the AI gives a verified answer.
Simple Analogy Instead of expecting a lawyer to memorize every law book (Standard LLM), you give them a search engine to pull up the exact rule files needed before they write your document (RAG).

Interface Standardization

Model Context Protocol (MCP)

MCP is an open standard — a secure, universal bridge allowing AI models to safely read databases, local files, and computer applications.

  • Universal Connection: A single setup format so developers don't need custom code every time they link an AI to a new program.
  • Safety Isolation: Stops an AI agent from accidentally wiping a computer's file system.
Simple Analogy MCP is a universal USB port for AI. Instead of engineering a different cable to attach an AI to a database or calendar, MCP provides a standard plug that connects to anything safely.

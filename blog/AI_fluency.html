Navigating the AI Landscape

Welcome to TheSudoBank's guide on the foundational technologies shaping the future of artificial intelligence.

Core Concepts

Artificial Intelligence (AI): The broad science of creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence.

Machine Learning (ML): A subset of AI focused on algorithms that allow computers to learn from and make predictions based on data.

Deep Learning: A specialized subset of ML using multi-layered Neural Networks to model complex patterns in data.

Transformer: The underlying architecture that powers modern AI, enabling the processing of sequential data with high efficiency.

Large Language Models & Mechanics

  • Large Language Model (LLM): A model trained on vast text data to understand, generate, and manipulate human language.
  • Token: The basic unit of text processing (often words or sub-words) that models use to "read" input.
  • Prompt: The input instruction provided by a user to guide the model's output.
  • Context Window: The limit of information (tokens) a model can "remember" or consider at one time.
  • Inference: The process of a trained model generating an output based on a new input.
  • Temperature: A setting that controls the randomness or "creativity" of the model's output.
  • Hallucination: When an AI confidently generates information that is factually incorrect.

Training & Optimization

  • Pre-training: The initial stage where a model learns from massive, unlabeled datasets.
  • Fine-tuning: The process of training a pre-trained model further on a smaller, curated dataset for specific tasks.
  • RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback): Using human feedback to align the model’s behavior with human intent.
  • AI Alignment: The field of research ensuring AI systems behave in ways consistent with human values and goals.

Advanced Capabilities

  • Embeddings: Representing words or data as numerical vectors in a multi-dimensional space to capture semantic meaning.
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): A technique where the model fetches external, up-to-date data before generating an answer.
  • AI Agent: An autonomous AI system capable of using tools and taking actions to achieve complex goals.
  • Multimodal AI: Models capable of processing and generating multiple types of media, such as text, images, and audio.

Core Concepts

4 terms
Artificial Intelligence (AI) AI

The broad scientific discipline of creating systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence — reasoning, perception, learning, and decision-making.

foundational
Machine Learning (ML) ML

A subset of AI focused on algorithms that allow computers to learn from and make predictions based on data, without being explicitly programmed for every rule.

subset
Deep Learning DL

A specialised subset of ML using multi-layered Neural Networks to model complex, hierarchical patterns in large volumes of data.

subset
Transformer architecture

The underlying neural architecture that powers modern AI, enabling efficient processing of sequential data through self‑attention mechanisms — the backbone of LLMs.

architecture
⚙️

Large Language Models & Mechanics

6 terms
Large Language Model (LLM) LLM

A model trained on vast text corpora to understand, generate, and manipulate human language with remarkable fluency and contextual awareness.

model
Token token

The basic unit of text processing — often words, sub‑words, or characters — that models use to "read" and encode input sequences.

mechanics
Prompt prompt

The input instruction or query provided by a user to guide the model's output. Prompt engineering is a critical skill for effective AI interaction.

interaction
Context Window context

The maximum number of tokens a model can "remember" or consider at one time — a key constraint for long‑form reasoning and document analysis.

limitation
Inference inference

The process of a trained model generating an output (prediction, text, or decision) based on a new, unseen input — the "run time" phase.

operation
Temperature temp

A hyperparameter that controls the randomness or "creativity" of the model's output. Lower values yield deterministic, focused responses; higher values encourage diversity.

control
Hallucination artifact

When an AI confidently generates information that is factually incorrect, fabricated, or inconsistent with the source data — a critical challenge in deployment.

risk alignment
🔬

Training & Optimization

4 terms
Pre‑training pre-train

The initial stage where a model learns language structure and world knowledge from massive, unlabeled datasets — the foundation of all modern LLMs.

phase
Fine‑tuning fine-tune

The process of training a pre‑trained model further on a smaller, curated dataset for specific tasks or domains, adapting its capabilities.

phase
RLHF Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback

Using human preference signals to align the model's behaviour with human intent, improving safety, helpfulness, and tone.

alignment
AI Alignment alignment

The field of research dedicated to ensuring AI systems behave in ways consistent with human values, goals, and ethical principles.

safety
🚀

Advanced Capabilities

4 terms
Embeddings vectors

Numerical representations of words, phrases, or data points in a multi‑dimensional space that capture semantic meaning and relationships.

representation
Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) RAG

A technique where the model fetches external, up‑to‑date data from a knowledge base before generating an answer — grounding responses in verifiable facts.

grounding
AI Agent agent

An autonomous AI system capable of using tools, planning sequences, and taking actions to achieve complex, multi‑step goals with minimal human oversight.

autonomy
Multimodal AI multimodal

Models capable of processing and generating multiple types of media — text, images, audio, video — enabling richer, more context‑aware interactions.

capability
